Lesson 2: Ransomware Safety

Ransomware is malicious software that can encrypt files and demand payment. An unexpected attachment or download can be dangerous.

Lesson 3: Password Protection

  • Use long, unique passwords for each account.
  • Use a reputable password manager.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Never share verification codes with strangers.
  • Change compromised passwords promptly.
